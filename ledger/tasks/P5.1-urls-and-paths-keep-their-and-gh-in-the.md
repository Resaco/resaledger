# P5.1 URLs and paths keep their # and gh- in the CI comment

- Status: doing
- Lane: p-review
- Evidence: —

## Plan

From [the review](../evidence/p/review.md), finding 7. In `ciMarkdownEscape`, a `#` inside a URL (a token with `://`) gets no joiner, so the URL's fragment stays part of GitHub's autolink, and `GH-<digits>` is matched only in capitals and at a word start that is not part of a path or slug.

Acceptance: `https://host/page#12` stays whole in the comment, `ledger/tasks/gh-2-fix.md` gets no joiner, and `#12`, `owner/repo#12`, `GH-12` and `@name` are still neutralised.
